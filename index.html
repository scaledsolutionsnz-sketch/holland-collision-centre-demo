About us

Rolleston's panel and paint people

Holland Collision Centre is a Rolleston-based collision repair shop looking after cars, utes and light trucks across Selwyn and Canterbury. From insurance claims to private cash jobs, we treat every repair like it's our own vehicle.

We keep you in the loop the whole way through, a clear quote up front, honest timelines, and a finished job that's straight, colour-matched and cleaned before you pick it up.

  • All insurance claims handledWe deal with your insurer directly, from assessment to sign-off.
