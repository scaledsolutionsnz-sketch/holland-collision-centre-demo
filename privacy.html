Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a placeholder privacy policy provided as part of a website demonstration. Holland Collision Centre should replace this content with a policy reviewed for its own business before going live.

Holland Collision Centre ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect the personal information you share with us, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Vehicle details and information about the repair or damage.
  • Insurance and claim information where a repair is carried out under a claim.
  • Any information you provide when you call or email us.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to prepare quotes, carry out repairs, communicate with you about your vehicle, liaise with your insurer where relevant, and process payments. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing your information

We may share information with your insurer, parts suppliers or sub-contractors only as needed to complete your repair. We may also share information where required by law.

4. Storing your information

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, and we take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

5. This website

This site links to a Google Maps embed and loads Google Fonts, which may collect standard technical data (such as your IP address) under Google's own privacy policy. Links to Google and social media pages open on those third-party platforms.

6. Your rights

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to request corrections. To do so, contact us using the details below.

7. Contact us

For any privacy questions, call 03 347 8703 or visit us at 826 Jones Road, Rolleston 7675.
